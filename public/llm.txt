# AEO Tracker — AI Engine Optimization & Brand Visibility

**URL:** https://aeo-tracker-navy.vercel.app

## What is AEO Tracker?

AEO Tracker is a SaaS platform that monitors and improves brand visibility in AI-generated answers. As AI search engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude become primary discovery tools, brands need to understand how — and whether — they appear in AI responses. AEO Tracker is the SEMrush for the AI era.

## Key Features

- **Brand Visibility Tracking:** Measures how often your brand appears in AI-generated answers and where it ranks relative to competitors.
- **Citation Tracking:** Identifies which URLs and sources AI engines cite when discussing your brand or category.
- **Share of Voice:** Compares your brand's AI mention rate against competitors across topics and queries.
- **Competitor Benchmarking:** Side-by-side analysis of sentiment, ranking position, and mention frequency vs. competing brands.
- **Topical Authority Map:** Shows topics where AI engines trust your brand and gaps where competitors dominate.
- **Accuracy Monitoring:** Flags inaccurate, outdated, or risky claims AI engines make about your brand.
- **AI Crawler Visibility:** Checks whether AI search crawlers can access your critical pages.

## How It Works

1. Create a project and add your brand plus competitors.
2. Add the queries (buying questions, category terms) you want to track.
3. AEO Tracker runs those queries across ChatGPT (GPT-4o), Google Gemini, and Anthropic Claude.
4. Results are parsed for mentions, ranking position, sentiment, and citations.
5. Track trends over time with daily or weekly scheduled runs.

## About AEO (AI Engine Optimization)

AEO is the discipline of optimizing digital presence for AI-generated answers rather than traditional search rankings. Where SEO targets crawlers and algorithms, AEO targets large language models — optimizing for citations, factual accuracy, structured data, and topical authority signals that influence how AI engines present your brand.
